## Site Survey

### 1. Tracksmith — The Journal
tracksmith.com/journal

**Masthead/header:** "The Journal" primary label with tagline "Inspirations On & Off the Track." No issue numbers. Categorical tabs (All Posts / Stories / Lookbooks / Films) sit immediately below — these act as the navigation spine rather than a traditional masthead date stamp. Footer anchors brand to a physical address (285 Newbury St., Boston), reinforcing heritage credibility.

**Featured post pattern:** Full-bleed hero for Films category; Stories use a card-forward layout. The section heading "Stories _that_ Inform" uses selective italics inside the heading string as a typographic contrast device — not a font switch, just `<em>` embedded mid-phrase.

**Archive layout:** Categorical tab navigation controls the view. Content is modular by type (text-heavy Stories vs. image-heavy Lookbooks vs. embedded Films), so no single grid rhythm governs all. Within Stories, cards appear to run 2-col on desktop.

**Card anatomy:** Image-first, title below, no visible dates or author credits on archive cards. The brand treats the image as the hook and the headline as the payoff — meta information is suppressed at the index level.

**Typography hierarchy:** Serif-inflected italic for section descriptors inside sans-serif headings. Newsletter CTA uses italic emphasis ("_Join Our_ Newsletter") — the same device used throughout, creating a consistent typographic signature.

**Navigation/filtering:** Four category tabs, no search, no tag pills on the index. Clean and opinionated — you browse by format type, not topic.

**In-article treatment:** [403 blocked on article pages] — Based on index structure, expect full-bleed hero at top, title below image (clean stack), editorial body with generous white space.

**Navigation/filtering:** Full category filter bar at top. Most robust filtering seen in survey. No search visible.

### 5. Buck Mason — The Journal
buckmason.com/journal + buckmason.com/blogs/journal

**Masthead/header:** "The Journal" — clean, minimal. Tagline: "Modern American Classics." No issue numbers, no date stamps on the index. The brand's promise sits in the navigation tagline, not a masthead eyebrow.

**Featured post pattern:** Large hero tile (first card in grid), full-bleed ~1:1 square crop. Text positioned **below image** — clean stack, no overlay. "Read More" CTA link beneath.

**Archive layout:** Multi-column grid, 2-3 columns on desktop. Consistent 1:1 square card aspect ratio throughout. Generous inter-card spacing. Infinite scroll or load-more (no visible pagination).

**Card anatomy:** 1:1 square image → bold title (large, 18-24px range) → 1-2 line descriptor in italics → no dates, no author, no reading time. Category filter pills appear above the grid: "all / collaborations / interviews / lookbooks / stories" — lowercase, minimal.

**Typography hierarchy:** Uppercase masthead treatment. Card titles: bold large sans-serif. Descriptors: italicized secondary text. Meta: suppressed entirely at index level. The brand's copy standard is "150 characters maximum" for card descriptions — extreme restraint.

**Navigation/filtering:** 5 category pills, no search. Simple and curated.

**In-article treatment (California Denim article):** Full-bleed hero → title "CALIFORNIA DENIM" uppercase below hero → narrative paragraph body → section headers as visual breaks → product CTAs integrated throughout. No pull quotes, no newsletter CTA within article. No byline or date in article view either. Product integration is the conversion architecture — editorial content and shop content are intentionally blurred.

**Key observation:** Buck Mason deliberately strips all meta (dates, authors, reading time) from both index and article views. This creates a **timeless** quality — nothing feels old. Editorial and product content are treated identically in the grid, normalizing commerce within editorial space.

## Synthesis

### 5 Patterns We Should Steal

**1. Rapha: Full-bleed title overlay on the featured hero**
The lead story on Rapha's journal doesn't stack title below image — it fuses them. The headline prints directly over the image at high contrast. This is the single strongest signal that "this is editorial, not a blog." For AO: apply this to the featured post on the /blog index only. Keep the text position bottom-left, consistent with the existing left-aligned editorial voice.

**2. Tracksmith: Selective italic emphasis mid-heading**
"Stories _that_ Inform" — one italicized word inside a sans-serif heading string. This costs nothing to implement but creates a typographic signature that feels art-directed rather than templated. For AO: apply to the journal masthead title or section dividers. Example: "The Word _and_ the Work" with "and" in italic.

**3. Rapha: Series/episode labels as issue numbers**
"The Rapha Roadmap: Part Eight" functions as an editorial series signal — readers know they're in an ongoing narrative. For AO: add optional `series` and `seriesNumber` fields to the Sanity schema. Render as `// SERIES TITLE — PART 0N` in the existing mono eyebrow style above the card title. This builds archive depth without redesigning anything.

**4. Buck Mason: Suppress all meta on index cards, let title + image carry the card**
Buck Mason shows no dates, no authors, no reading time on any card. Everything feels current. The title and a one-line descriptor do all the work. For AO: move date to a secondary position (or suppress it entirely on the grid view), remove author from cards, let the mono eyebrow tag replace both. "// FAITH · 4 MIN READ" or simply "// TRAINING" is enough meta for a grid card.

**5. Buck Mason: Normalize editorial and product in the same visual grammar**
Buck Mason runs lookbooks and stories through the same card grid at the same visual weight — you can't tell which is editorial and which is commerce until you click. For AO: the blog index should feel like it could contain a product drop post or a scripture reflection post in the same visual tier. When AO eventually publishes a "New Drop: Cornerstone Tee" blog post, it should not look like a Shopify product listing — it should look like an editorial beat.

---

### 3 Patterns to Avoid

**1. The title-below-title duplicate (current AO grid cards do this)**
The existing AO /blog grid renders the title once inside the image (as overlay) and again below the image as standalone text — the same string appears twice in the same card. This is a Medium/Substack pattern that breaks the editorial voice. Fix: pick one location. Recommended: title only below the image, no overlay on grid cards (save overlay for the featured hero only).

**2. Centered masthead on the list page**
The current AO masthead is fully center-aligned — text center, title center, descriptor center. Every reference site surveyed uses **left-aligned** mastheads. Centered layout reads as blog/Substack; left-aligned reads as newspaper/journal. The existing `// THE JOURNAL` mono eyebrow is strong — it just needs to be left-aligned with a left rule or border treatment beneath it.

**3. Tags as the primary meta signal on index cards**
The current grid cards show full tag arrays (multiple pill tags per card) below the title. At small type, this reads as visual noise and dilutes the card's typographic hierarchy. Reference sites use at most one category signal per card, rendered as a single eyebrow text above the title, not as pill arrays below it. Fix: replace the multi-tag pill array on grid cards with a single primary tag rendered as the mono eyebrow (`// TAG NAME`) above the title.

## Recommended Layout Structure

### /blog (List Page)

- **Page-level masthead** (left-aligned, not centered):
  - `// THE WORD AND THE WORK` — mono eyebrow at 11-12px, tracking-widest, white/40
  - `THE JOURNAL` — Outfit Black, ~72px, left-aligned, flush to left margin
  - Single horizontal rule full-width below masthead block
  - No tagline body text needed — the heading is enough

- **Featured post (latest):**
  - Full-bleed hero, `aspect-[3/2]` (wider than current 21/9 — more editorial, less cinematic)
  - Title printed **over** the image at bottom-left with `text-shadow` or semi-opaque mask strip only beneath the text (not a full gradient overlay)
  - Single eyebrow above title: `// TAG — MONTH YYYY` in mono
  - No excerpt text on the featured hero — title does the work
  - "Read —" arrow link bottom-right, same mono treatment

- **Divider row:**
  - Full-width rule + `// RECENT ENTRIES` eyebrow left-aligned

- **Archive grid:**
  - 3-col on desktop (not 2-col) — tighter, more newspaper-like
  - `aspect-[4/3]` images — more editorial than widescreen
  - Title ONLY below image — no overlay on grid cards
  - Single mono eyebrow above title: `// PRIMARY TAG`
  - Date in mono below title (suppress author at this level)
  - No excerpt on grid cards — forces stronger titles
  - No tag pill arrays

- **Load more / pagination:**
  - Mono text link "// LOAD MORE ENTRIES →" — no button component

---

### /blog/[slug] (Post Page)

- **Breadcrumb:** `← Back to Journal` — keep existing mono treatment, left-aligned

- **Post header block (full-width, left-aligned):**
  - Single primary tag as mono eyebrow: `// TAG NAME`
  - If series: `// SERIES TITLE — PART 0N` on its own line
  - H1 title: Outfit Black, ~60-72px on desktop, **left-aligned** (not centered as it currently is) — editorial journals are left-aligned
  - Date + author on one mono line: `MAY 13, 2026 — BY AUTHOR NAME` — no "By" prefix, no dot separator
  - Full-width rule below header block

- **Hero image:**
  - Full-bleed, `aspect-[3/1]` (cinematic banner ratio) — wide and immersive
  - No text overlay on the article hero (save overlay for the index featured card)

- **Body column:**
  - Keep existing `max-w-[720px]` — correct editorial column width
  - Body text: `text-lg leading-8` — keep current (good)
  - Blockquotes: increase left border to `border-l-4`, increase contrast slightly (`border-white/60`), remove italic — pull quotes should feel like a block, not a whisper
  - In-body images: full-width to column edge with 8-12px mono caption below (`// CAPTION TEXT`)

- **Scripture / featured verse module:**
  - Add a dedicated Portable Text block type for scripture references
  - Styled as: centered, Outfit Black italic, ~28px, white/80, no border — floats as a statement in the column
  - Reference line below in mono: `— BOOK CHAPTER:VERSE`

- **End-of-article block:**
  - Full-width rule
  - Product CTA (optional): `// GEAR WORN IN THIS TRAINING` — image + product name + link, 2-col
  - Newsletter CTA strip: simple mono label `// STAY IN IT` + email input, full-width bg-white/5 panel
  - Share row: mono links only, no social icons

- **Related posts:**
  - Keep 3-col grid
  - Remove title overlay from related post thumbnails — title below only
  - Add mono eyebrow `// MORE FROM THE JOURNAL` (keep existing — it's correct)

## Typography Recommendations

AO already uses Outfit (sans) + monospace (eyebrows). These are the right choices. The gaps are in sizing discipline and alignment.

**Masthead (journal page title):** Outfit Black, 64-80px, tracking-tight, left-aligned. Currently set at 5xl/6xl centered — needs left alignment and slight size increase.

**Featured hero title (overlay):** Outfit Black, 40-56px, tracking-tight. White, no text-shadow — use a masked strip (gradient only behind the text line, not full image overlay).

**Grid card titles:** Outfit Bold (700), 18-20px, tracking-tight. Currently duplicated (overlay + below) — pick below only.

**Mono eyebrows:** Keep `font-mono text-[11px] uppercase tracking-[0.18em] text-white/40`. This is already correct. The only change: move them **above** the title on grid cards (currently they appear below or alongside meta), and add the `// TAG — DATE` compound format.

**Article H1:** Outfit Black, 56-72px, **left-aligned**. Currently centered — this is the most impactful single change for editorial credibility.

**Article body:** `text-lg leading-8 text-white/80` — keep exactly as is. This is well-calibrated.

**Pull quotes / blockquotes:** Remove italic, increase contrast to `text-white/70`, add `border-l-4 border-white/50`. Currently too quiet — they need to feel like a statement pause, not a footnote.

**Scripture verse module (new):** Outfit Black Italic, ~28-32px, centered within the 720px column, `text-white/85`. Reference in mono below: `text-[11px] tracking-[0.18em] text-white/40`.

**Series/issue labels (new):** Mono, `text-[10px] tracking-[0.2em] uppercase text-white/30` — smallest level in the hierarchy, appears above the primary mono eyebrow. Creates a two-level eyebrow system: `// TRAINING SERIES — PART 03` on line one, `// TRAINING` as the primary tag on line two.
