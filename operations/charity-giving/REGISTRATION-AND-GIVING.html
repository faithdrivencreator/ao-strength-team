THE 10% TITHE - GIVING PARTNER GUIDE

MONTHLY GIVING ROUTINE Run / read / give / save / repeat
1
Run the ledger from the repo root for the month being closed. node scripts/tithe-ledger.mjs --since YYYY-MM-01
2
Open the statement in operations/tithe-statements/. It shows the 10% owed to each charity from that period's Stripe sales.
3
Donate each listed amount at its direct URL above. Card or ACH. No accounts required for one-time gifts.
4
Save each receipt next to the ledger as YYYY-MM-org-receipt.pdf (e.g. 2026-06-compassion-receipt.pdf).
5
Repeat monthly or quarterly. All four support one-time and recurring giving. No penalty for batching.
Every Gift Is Pete's To Complete

No accounts, subscriptions, or payments are created automatically. Each business signup and each donation is initiated by hand. Pete controls the timing, the amount, and the charity split for every period.
